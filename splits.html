💰 0xSplits Revenue Distribution

Trustless on-chain fee splitting for $SOUP Kitchen

// THE PROBLEM

$SOUP generates WETH/USDC trading fees on Base L2. Currently, the 40/30/30 revenue split is promise-based — Skarlun claims fees manually and distributes to team wallets. This works with trust, but doesn't scale.

⚠️ Important: The 0xSplits contract covers WETH/USDC trading fees only, NOT $SOUP tokens themselves. $SOUP token ownership is separate from fee distribution.

// THE SOLUTION

0xSplits enables trustless, immutable revenue distribution via smart contracts on Base. Once deployed, fees flow automatically to each team member's wallet. No manual claims, no trust required.
